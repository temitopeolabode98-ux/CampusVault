NOT IMPLEMENTED

There is no login form here on purpose.

CampusVault has no account system and no server, so signing in is not possible. No login form is shown because nothing would happen if you submitted one, and no password is ever asked for or stored on this device.

You do not need an account to use the site. Everything already available — browsing, searching, filtering, saving resources, and recently viewed items — works without signing in, and your saved items stay in your own browser.
