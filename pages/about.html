About

About CampusVault

A digital academic library that helps students find and study course materials in one place.

What CampusVault is

CampusVault brings scattered academic materials together into a single, searchable library. Instead of hunting through different folders, chats, or drives for a lecture note or a past question, a student can browse by course, filter by resource type, level, or academic session, and open the material directly.

The goal is simple: spend less time looking for materials and more time studying them.

WHO IT IS FOR

Designed for students first

Primary

Students

Students are the main audience. CampusVault helps them browse available materials by course and resource type, save items for later, and revisit what they last looked at while preparing for coursework and examinations.

Secondary

Lecturers and academic staff

Lecturers and academic staff can also use CampusVault to see how materials are organised and shared with students. Note that this site does not yet offer an upload or publishing tool for staff.

MATERIALS

What you can find

CampusVault is intended to help users access academic materials such as:

  • Lecture notes
  • Past examination questions
  • Assignments and coursework
  • Other course-related documents

Each material is linked to its course, level, and academic session, so it is clear what a document covers before you open it.

HOW IT WORKS

Browse and study

Step 1

Browse or search

Start from the Resources page to search by course code or keyword, or browse by course on the Courses page.

Step 2

Filter and open

Narrow results by resource type, level, or academic session, then open a material to read its details and, where a file is available, view the document.

Step 3

Save and revisit

Save resources to come back to them later, and see what you recently viewed. Saved items and history are stored in your own browser.

HONEST STATUS

What works today, and what is planned

Available now

  • Browsing and searching the existing resource catalogue by course code, title, or keyword.
  • Filtering by resource type, level, and academic session.
  • Viewing resource details, and opening a document where a file is actually available.
  • Saving resources and tracking recently viewed items, stored locally in your browser.
  • A light and dark mode that follows your preference.

Not available yet

  • Real user accounts. There is no sign-up or sign-in system, and the Login and Register pages are honest placeholders.
  • Lecturer uploads or publishing tools.
  • Server-side storage. Saved items and history live only in the browser you are using.
  • A backend that could deliver messages, sync data across devices, or authenticate anyone.

These items are described as future capabilities, not as features that already work. Nothing on this site pretends to be connected to a server when it is not.
